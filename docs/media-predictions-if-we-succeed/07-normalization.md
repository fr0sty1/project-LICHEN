# Phase 7: Normalization (Month 12+)

**Trigger:** Time passes, nothing catastrophic happens, the technology becomes boring

## The Pattern

Technologies that survive the backlash cycle without regulatory intervention or catastrophic misuse incidents eventually become "normal." They stop being news and start being infrastructure.

This is the goal. Interesting technology is vulnerable technology. Boring technology is stable technology.

## Coverage Pattern

**Outdoor/Adventure Publications:**
> "Off-Grid Communication for Backcountry Safety"
> LICHEN mentioned alongside satellite messengers
> No explanation of what it is (assumed reader knowledge)
> Affiliate links to hardware

**Maker/DIY Publications:**
> "How to Build Your Own Emergency Mesh Network"
> Step-by-step tutorial
> Assumes no controversy
> Comments are helpful rather than argumentative

**Mainstream How-To:**
> "5 Ways to Stay Connected During a Power Outage"
> LICHEN is item 3 or 4
> One paragraph explanation
> No mention of politics or controversy

**Enterprise/Government:**
> "Mesh Networks for Emergency Management: A Primer"
> White paper format
> Discusses procurement and integration
> LICHEN mentioned as "open-source option"

## Indicators of Normalization

- Hardware available at mainstream retailers
- No longer requires explanation in headlines
- Mentioned in emergency preparedness guides without caveats
- Amateur radio clubs offer LICHEN workshops
- Local emergency management agencies have evaluation reports
- "LICHEN" is a common noun, not a proper noun requiring definition

## What Normalized Coverage Looks Like

Before normalization:
> "LICHEN, an open-source mesh networking protocol that uses LoRa radio..."

After normalization:
> "Set up a LICHEN node for backup communication during..."

The technology has become self-explanatory to the relevant audience.

## What This Means

**Positive:**
- Regulatory attention decreases
- User base stabilizes and grows
- Development can focus on features rather than advocacy
- The project becomes sustainable

**Negative:**
- Harder to attract new contributors (boring isn't exciting)
- Harder to get press coverage (no longer novel)
- Funding harder to obtain (not "innovative" anymore)
- Competitors can copy without attribution pressure

## The End State

LICHEN becomes like ham radio, Linux, or Tor: a technology that experts know about, that serves its users well, and that most people have vaguely heard of but don't think about.

This is success.

The alternative — remaining perpetually "interesting" — means perpetual controversy, perpetual regulatory threat, and perpetual instability. Boring is better.

## Timeline

Full normalization takes 2-3 years from mainstream awareness. This document is written at Month 0 (August 2026). By Month 24-36, LICHEN will be boring.

We look forward to it.

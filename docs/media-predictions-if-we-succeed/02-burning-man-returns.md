# Phase 2: Burning Man Returns (Week 3-4)

**Trigger:** Festival attendees return with stories and photos

## Coverage Pattern

The Burning Man angle will surface through social media first, then get picked up by outlets that cover "internet culture" and alternative lifestyles. The framing will shift from technical to experiential.

## Expected Headlines (Structural Templates)

**Internet Culture Outlet:**
> "Inside the Off-Grid Mesh Network That Took Over Burning Man"
> "How [Festival] Built Its Own Phone Network With $30 Radios"

**Tech Lifestyle Outlet:**
> "This tiny radio could change how [group] communicates"
> Will pivot to protest/activism angle regardless of our actual use cases

**Photography-Heavy Outlet:**
> Dusty hardware strapped to art cars
> Rangers holding radios
> "The playa's unofficial infrastructure"

## Narrative Structure

1. **Scene-setting:** No cell service in the desert, 80,000 people
2. **Discovery:** Someone brought these weird radios
3. **Adoption:** By Wednesday, everyone wanted one
4. **Utility:** Rangers used it, Medical used it, someone got found
5. **Implication:** What if we had this everywhere?

## The "Found Person" Story

There will be a story about mesh-assisted location of a lost/injured person. This will become the emotional anchor for subsequent coverage. The details will vary but the structure will be:

- Person in distress
- Cell phones useless
- Mesh network relayed the alert
- Happy ending

This story will be referenced in 80% of subsequent mainstream coverage.

## What They Will Emphasize

- The aesthetic (dusty radios, DIY ethos)
- The community aspect (。neighbors helping neighbors")
- The "worked when nothing else did" angle
- Implicit critique of cell carrier dependence

## What They Will Underplay

- The technical details (too boring for this audience)
- The months of development (doesn't fit "organic emergence" narrative)
- The HAM radio legal context (too complicated)
- That it's a reflash of existing hardware (less romantic than "built their own")

## The Pivot

Lifestyle outlets will inevitably ask: "What else could this be used for?"

The answer they want: protests, disasters, surveillance resistance.

This sets up Phase 3.

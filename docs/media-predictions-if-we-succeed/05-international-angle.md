# Phase 5: The International Angle (Month 6)

**Trigger:** LICHEN used during internet blackout in authoritarian context

## The Pattern

Governments shut down internet access during protests, elections, or unrest. This happens multiple times per year in various countries. Eventually, one of these events will involve LICHEN.

We do not control where this happens. We observe that it will happen somewhere.

## Coverage Pattern

**Wire Service (First Report):**
> "Protesters turn to amateur radio mesh after internet blackout"
> Careful, factual, attributes claims
> Notes the technology is "dual-use"
> Quotes from human rights organization

**Prestige Newspaper (Follow-Up):**
> "How a hobby radio project became essential infrastructure for dissidents"
> Anonymous sources
> Historical context (Arab Spring, earlier mesh attempts)
> Expert commentary on censorship circumvention
> Implicit framing: technology as liberation

**Activist-Adjacent Publication:**
> Emphasizes the "can't be shut down" angle
> Interviews with people on the ground (pseudonymous)
> Detailed technical explanation aimed at replication
> Links to resources

**State-Aligned Media (Adversarial Framing):**
> "Foreign-developed communication tool used by [unflattering term for protesters]"
> Implies outside coordination
> May describe as "spy technology" or similar
> We become a geopolitical prop

## The Narrative Fork

Coverage will split based on outlet's relationship to the state in question:

**Outlets critical of that state:**
> Technology enables freedom, brave dissidents, authoritarian overreach

**Outlets aligned with that state:**
> Technology enables terrorism, foreign interference, legitimate security concerns

**Neutral/wire services:**
> Technology exists, was used, here are the facts, no explicit framing

We have no control over which framing dominates. All three will exist simultaneously.

## What This Changes

Before this phase: LICHEN is a "cool tech project"
After this phase: LICHEN is a "political technology"

This is irreversible. The project will be permanently associated with censorship resistance regardless of our intentions or statements.

## Our Response Options

1. **Embrace it:** Lean into the "freedom technology" framing
2. **Distance:** Emphasize technical neutrality, refuse political framing
3. **Silence:** Make no public statements, let others define the narrative

All options have costs. Silence is often interpreted as assent to whatever framing dominates.

## What We Cannot Predict

- Which country
- Which specific event
- Whether people are hurt
- Whether our technology actually helped or was incidental
- Whether governments attempt to regulate LoRa frequencies in response

## What We Can Predict

- The story structure will match the templates above
- We will be asked to comment
- Whatever we say will be quoted selectively
- The "what about bad actors" concern will intensify (see Phase 6)

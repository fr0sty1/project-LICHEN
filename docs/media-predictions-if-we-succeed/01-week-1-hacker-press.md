# Phase 1: Hacker Press (Week 1-2)

**Trigger:** DEF CON demo, working firmware on existing hardware

## Coverage Pattern

Technical publications will cover LICHEN based on technical merit. The framing will emphasize:

- "Finally done right" - Comparison to Meshtastic's technical limitations
- Standards compliance as virtue - IPv6, CoAP, SCHC as legitimacy markers
- Runs on existing hardware - The "just reflash" angle
- Open source done properly - GPL-3.0, specs available, no vendor lock-in

## Expected Headlines (Structural Templates)

**Technical Deep-Dive Outlet:**
> "LICHEN: The mesh protocol that treats you like an adult"
> "What if [existing project] and [standard] had a baby"

**Maker/Hardware Outlet:**
> Posts the schematic, says "finally"
> Minimal editorial, maximum technical detail

**Security-Focused Outlet:**
> Leads with the crypto: Schnorr signatures, TOFU model
> Will quote or reference a well-known cryptographer on the trust model
> Framing: "paranoid but practical"

## Characteristic Elements

- Code snippets in the article
- Architecture diagrams reproduced from our docs
- "I flashed my existing device and it worked" testimonials
- Comparison tables (LICHEN vs Meshtastic vs LoRaWAN)
- Comments section full of "why not just use X" that the article already answered

## What They Will Get Wrong

- Confusing SCHC with 6LoWPAN
- Saying "blockchain" when they mean "cryptographic signatures"
- Implying we invented the underlying standards rather than assembled them
- Screenshots of the wrong hardware

## What They Will Get Right

- The technical differentiation from Meshtastic
- The cost comparison to commercial alternatives
- The "no infrastructure required" angle
- That it actually works

## Duration

Two weeks of technical coverage, then silence until the next phase trigger.
